## Inspection

> ⚠ The load cell is a core component of the crane's overload protection system. Do not operate the crane with a signal fault present.

- [ ] Confirm power is OFF
- [ ] Check connector and cable for damage
- [ ] Check load cell body for deformation/damage
- [ ] Check mounting pin and bracket tightness
- [ ] Confirm zero signal is normal (with no load)
- [ ] Check overload indicator history
- [ ] Check cable sheath and waterproof connector condition

### Acceptance Criteria

| Item | NORMAL | ABNORMAL |
|---|---|---|
| Appearance | No deformation | Bending, cracks, corrosion |
| Zero Signal | Stable within spec | Drift, excessive noise |
| Overload Indicator | Not triggered | Triggered (requires replacement/precision inspection) |

> ✅ If the overload indicator has been triggered, precision calibration and structural inspection are required before deciding whether the unit can be reused.
