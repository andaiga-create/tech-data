# Inspection

> ⚠ **Heavy Load** — Do not inspect pin-type load cell with load suspended.

## Checklist

- [ ] No load on hook during inspection
- [ ] Power OFF confirmed before opening junction box
- [ ] Connector/cable gland sealed, no water ingress signs
- [ ] Cable undamaged along full run to junction box
- [ ] Load cell pin/body free of visible mechanical damage, corrosion, or paint chips at strain gauge area
- [ ] Mounting/retaining hardware secure (pin retaining plate, bolts)
- [ ] Zero reading with no load matches expected baseline (see **Setting**)
- [ ] LMI display shows stable reading (no flicker/jump) with static test load

## Result

`Normal` / `Abnormal` — record zero-load reading and compare to last calibration record.

## Notes

A drifting or noisy zero reading with no load applied usually points to moisture ingress at the cable gland or a damaged strain gauge — do not use for lifting until verified.
