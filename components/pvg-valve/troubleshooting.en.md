## Troubleshooting

Search keywords: PVG Valve, Spool Stuck, No Response, Overload, LS Pressure

### Symptom: A specific section does not respond (no movement despite joystick input)

**Possible Cause**
- PVEH coil open circuit
- No signal output from amplifier card
- Spool stuck (contamination ingress)

**Inspection**
- [ ] Measure coil resistance
- [ ] Measure amplifier card output current while operating the joystick
- [ ] Check whether manual override allows manual operation

**Corrective Action**
1. Replace the coil if open circuit
2. If the amplifier card outputs no signal, check the card and wiring
3. If the spool is stuck, disassemble and clean the section (also check oil contamination)

**Final Check**
Verify linear response by operating the joystick across the full range.

---

### Symptom: A specific section slows down during combined operation

**Possible Cause**
- Compensator malfunction
- LS line pressure loss
- Insufficient pump flow (combined demand exceeds supply)

**Inspection**
- [ ] Check compensator spool for sticking
- [ ] Verify normal speed during single-section operation (to distinguish from a pump flow issue)

**Corrective Action**
1. Clean or replace the compensator
2. If pump flow is insufficient, adjust the work sequence or review pump sizing

> ⚠ When disassembling the compensator, always record spring direction and order before starting work.
