## Overview

The Encoder is an electrical component that detects the rotational position and speed of the crane hoist/slewing drive shaft and feeds it back to the control panel (PLC).

This document is based on the **Heidenhain E80H/E100 series** and is directly involved in position control (positioning) and speed control (overspeed prevention).

> ℹ Encoder signal faults can directly cause a crane E-Stop or abnormal operation. Always follow the Inspection procedure below before servicing.

### Key Functions

- Rotational position (angle) detection
- Rotational speed detection and overspeed monitoring
- Feedback signal to PLC/inverter (A/B/Z pulse or SSI)

### Mounting Location

Installed at the rear of the motor or on the gearbox input shaft via a flexible coupling.
