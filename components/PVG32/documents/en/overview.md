# PVG 32 Overview

## Product
PVG 32 is a hydraulic Load Sensing Proportional Valve Group. Its modular architecture supports configurations from a simple LS directional valve to an electrically controlled load-independent proportional valve.

## Key Features
- Load-independent flow control
- Good regulation characteristics
- Energy saving
- Up to 12 PVB basic modules
- Several connection thread options
- Compact design

## Main Modules
PVP(Pump Side), PVB(Basic), PVBS(Main Spool), PVE(Electrical), PVM(Mechanical), PVH(Hydraulic), PVPC(External Pilot), PVPX(LS Unloading), PVLP/PVLA(Protection), PVS/PVSI(End Plate), PVAS(Assembly Kit).

## Field Key Point
Do not identify the complete PVG 32 only by its external appearance. The final configuration is determined by the combination of PVP + PVB + PVBS + Actuator + Protection Valves + End Plate.
