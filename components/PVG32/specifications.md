## Oil / Filtration
- Recommended mineral hydraulic oil: HLP(DIN 51524) / HM(ISO 6743/4)
- Maximum permitted contamination: ISO 4406 23/19/16
- High reliability systems: pressure filter with bypass and indicator
- Catalog experience: 10 μm nominal or finer, or 20 μm absolute or finer

## PVE
PVE 계열은 사양에 따라 11–32 V 또는 12/24 V 구성이 있으며, PVEA/H/S/U/P 및 PVED-CC/CX는 closed-loop spool control을 지원한다.
