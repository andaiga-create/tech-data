## Setting

| Item | Factory Default | Recommended | Adjustment Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| LS Pressure Margin | 18 bar | 18–20 bar | 15–25 bar |
| Max Pressure Cut-off | 350 bar | Determined by crane rating | 250–420 bar |
| Standby Pressure | LS Margin + load pressure | Auto-calculated | - |

### Setting Procedure

1. Warm up until hydraulic oil temperature reaches 40°C or above (normal operating temperature)
2. Connect a pressure gauge to the test port
3. Slowly turn the Max Pressure Cut-off adjustment screw clockwise until the target pressure is reached
4. Adjust the LS Pressure Margin screw to bring standby pressure to the target value
5. Verify pressure response from no-load to full-load conditions

> ⚠ Never set the Max Pressure Cut-off above the crane's rated value. Doing so can cause structural damage or an overload accident.

### Acceptance Criteria

Cut-off pressure within ±3% of setting, standby pressure stable (pulsation within ±1 bar).
