# Troubleshooting

**Keywords:** Hydraulic Pressure Low, Abnormal Noise, Leakage, No Flow, Overheating

---

## Case 1 — Hydraulic Pressure Low / Slow Function Speed

**Problem**
All functions run slower than normal, or crane fails to reach rated pressure under load.

**Possible Cause**
- Main relief valve stuck open or set too low
- Worn pump (internal slippage / low volumetric efficiency)
- Suction restriction (clogged filter, low oil, collapsed hose)
- Load-sensing signal line blocked or damaged

**Inspection**
1. Check standby pressure at test port (see **Setting**).
2. Check suction filter differential pressure indicator.
3. Check oil level and condition (aerated/milky oil indicates water ingress).
4. Isolate pump — check case drain flow rate against spec.

**Corrective Action**
- Clean/replace suction filter if restricted.
- Top up or replace oil if low/contaminated.
- If case drain flow exceeds spec at rated pressure → pump worn, replace/rebuild.
- Clear or replace load-sensing line if blocked.

**Final Check**
Confirm standby pressure and full-load pressure both within spec, function speed matches nameplate cycle time.

---

## Case 2 — Abnormal Noise (Cavitation / Whine)

**Problem**
Loud whining or knocking noise from pump, especially at startup or high RPM.

**Possible Cause**
- Air ingress on suction side (loose fitting, low oil)
- Suction line restriction / collapsed hose
- Worn bearings

**Inspection**
1. Check oil level and suction line fittings for tightness.
2. Check suction strainer for blockage.
3. Listen for change in noise when oil warms up (cavitation often reduces once oil viscosity drops).

**Corrective Action**
- Tighten/reseal suction fittings, top up oil.
- Replace clogged suction strainer.
- If noise persists after ruling out suction issues → internal bearing wear, schedule pump replacement.

**Final Check**
Run at rated RPM for 10 minutes, confirm noise level normal and case drain temperature stable.
