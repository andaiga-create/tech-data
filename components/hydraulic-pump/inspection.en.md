## Inspection

> ⚠ Always fully release hydraulic pressure before inspecting hydraulic lines. Disconnecting a hose under residual pressure can cause serious injury from high-pressure fluid injection.

- [ ] Confirm power OFF and hydraulic pressure fully released
- [ ] Check pump exterior for oil leakage
- [ ] Check suction line for air ingress
- [ ] Confirm pressure gauge reads correctly
- [ ] Check coupling and mounting bolt tightness
- [ ] Check for abnormal noise/vibration (check suction side first if cavitation is suspected)
- [ ] Check oil temperature and contamination (color, odor)

### Acceptance Criteria

| Item | NORMAL | ABNORMAL |
|---|---|---|
| Leakage | None (slight wetting at most) | Dripping or worse |
| Noise | Uniform running sound | Metallic knock, cavitation noise |
| Pressure | Within ±5% of setting | Deviation beyond setting |

> ✅ If any item is ABNORMAL, refer to the matching symptom in the Troubleshooting procedure.
