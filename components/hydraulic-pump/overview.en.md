## Overview

The hydraulic pump is the power source of the crane's hydraulic system, converting the rotational force of the engine/motor into hydraulic energy.

This document is based on the **Danfoss XPI 140 variable displacement piston pump**.

> ℹ The XPI series uses Load Sensing (LS), automatically adjusting discharge flow according to actual load demand.

### Key Functions

- Load-Sensing based automatic flow/pressure control
- Standby pressure mode for power saving when unloaded
- Supports simultaneous operation of multiple actuators
