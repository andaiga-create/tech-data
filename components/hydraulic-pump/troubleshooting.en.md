## Troubleshooting

Search keywords: Hydraulic Pressure Low, Pump Noise, Abnormal Noise, Leakage, Overheat

### Symptom: Hydraulic Pressure Low

**Possible Cause**
- LS line blocked or leaking
- Air ingress in suction line
- Relief valve setting has drifted
- Internal pump wear (reduced volumetric efficiency)

**Inspection**
- [ ] Measure LS line pressure (normal: load pressure + ~18 bar)
- [ ] Check suction line clamps/seals
- [ ] Re-verify relief valve setting pressure

**Corrective Action**
1. Clean or replace the LS line
2. Re-seat suction-side seals/clamps
3. Re-set the relief valve (see Setting procedure)
4. If unresolved, perform a volumetric efficiency test; replace the pump if below spec

**Final Check**
Confirm target pressure is reached and held under full-load conditions.

---

### Symptom: Abnormal Noise

**Possible Cause**
- Cavitation (poor suction)
- Coupling misalignment
- Internal component wear/damage

**Inspection**
- [ ] Measure suction line vacuum level
- [ ] Re-check coupling alignment
- [ ] Check oil filter for clogging

**Corrective Action**
1. Seal any air leak points in the suction line
2. Replace the oil filter
3. Realign the coupling

> ⚠ Prolonged operation under cavitation rapidly accelerates internal component damage.
