# Inspection

> ⚠ **Hydraulic Pressure** — Do not loosen any fitting until pressure is confirmed released.
> ⚠ **Rotating Parts** — Keep clear of coupling while engine/motor running.

## Checklist

- [ ] Power/engine OFF confirmed before physical inspection
- [ ] No external oil leakage at shaft seal, case drain, or ports
- [ ] Coupling guard installed and secure
- [ ] Mounting bolts tight, no abnormal vibration marks
- [ ] Suction hose free of collapse, cracks, or soft spots
- [ ] Case drain line clear, not kinked
- [ ] Oil level in reservoir within sight-glass range
- [ ] No abnormal noise (cavitation/whine) during start-up
- [ ] Case drain temperature within normal range after warm-up (< 80°C)

## Result

`Normal` / `Abnormal` — record readings and note any leak location precisely (port, torque value if re-tightened).

## Notes

Cavitation noise on startup usually indicates a restricted suction line or low oil level — check before running under load.
