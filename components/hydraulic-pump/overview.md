# Hydraulic Pump

**Manufacturer:** Rexroth &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Model:** A11VO130 / A11VO190 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Category:** Hydraulic

Variable displacement axial piston pump, main power source for boom hoist, luffing, slew, and winch circuits.

## Function

- Driven directly by diesel engine or electric motor via coupling
- Swash plate angle varies displacement to match load demand (load-sensing control)
- Supplies pressurized oil to PVG proportional valve bank

## Where to find it

Mounted on the engine bell housing / power pack frame, in the machinery room below the crane pedestal.

> ⚠ **Hydraulic Pressure / Stored Energy** — System pressure can exceed 350 bar. Relieve pressure and confirm gauge reads zero before disconnecting any line.

## Related components

- PVG proportional valve bank
- Load-sensing control module (on pump)
- Suction/return filters
- Oil cooler
