## Inspection

> ⚠ Before beginning inspection, be sure to disconnect the electric current by turning the power SWITCH OFF. Otherwise, a serious accident may result. (The same applies when making adjustments such as GAP adjustment.)

- [ ] Confirm power is OFF
- [ ] Check the GAP between MAGNET(1) and ARMATURE(2) around the full 360° circumference (spec: 0.5mm)
- [ ] Check the LINING(4) surface for oil or moisture contamination
- [ ] Check that GAP FIXING BOLT(5), GAP REGULATION SCREW(6), and TORQUE ADJUSTABLE BOLT(7) are properly tightened
- [ ] (Before trial run) Supply current to the magnet coil only and confirm the shaft rotates without resistance

### Acceptance Criteria

| Item | NORMAL | ABNORMAL |
|---|---|---|
| GAP | 0.5mm, uniform around 360° | Exceeds 0.5mm (gap widened) or uneven |
| LINING surface | Dry, no contamination | Contaminated with oil/moisture |
| Braking (de-energized) | Brakes immediately under spring pressure | Delayed or no braking |
| Energized (no-load trial run) | Shaft rotates smoothly without resistance | Binding, resistance, or abnormal noise |

> ℹ If the lining is contaminated with oil or moisture, it loses its braking function — always visually check for contamination even if other items pass.

> ✅ If all items are NORMAL, the unit may return to service. If any item is ABNORMAL, proceed to the Troubleshooting procedure.
