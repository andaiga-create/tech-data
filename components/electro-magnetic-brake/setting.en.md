## Setting (GAP Adjustment)

| Item | Factory Default | Recommended | Adjustment Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| GAP (MAGNET–ARMATURE) | Not stated in source material | 0.5mm (uniform around 360°) | Not stated in source material |

> ℹ The Factory Default and Adjustment Range (tolerance) are not specified in the provided material. Adjust to the 0.5mm spec value; verify the exact tolerance against the field nameplate or with the manufacturer.

### Setting Procedure

1. Confirm power is OFF before starting work (see the safety warning in the Inspection tab)
2. Loosen the four GAP FIXING BOLT(5) locations one at a time
3. Measure the GAP between MAGNET(1) and ARMATURE(2) around the full 360° circumference
4. Using the GAP REGULATION SCREW(6), adjust the gap until it is uniformly 0.5mm all the way around
5. Re-tighten the four GAP FIXING BOLT(5) locations evenly
6. Supply current to the MAGNET COIL only and confirm the shaft rotates without resistance (no-load trial run)
7. If no issues are found, perform a trial run under load
8. If SLIP (delayed/incomplete braking) occurs during use, it is caused by the gap widening — repeat steps 2–7 to re-adjust the gap to 0.5mm
9. To increase braking torque, turn the TORQUE ADJUSTABLE BOLT(7) clockwise (CW) little by little

> ⚠ Adjust the TORQUE ADJUSTABLE BOLT(7) in small increments and check braking condition after each adjustment, rather than tightening a large amount at once. Over-tightening can cause braking shock or component damage.

### Acceptance Criteria

- GAP measures a uniform 0.5mm around the full 360° circumference
- The shaft rotates smoothly without resistance or binding when the magnet coil is energized
- No SLIP (delayed braking) occurs during a loaded trial run
