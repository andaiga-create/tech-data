## Overview

The Electro Magnetic Brake is a fail-safe (de-energized-to-brake) DC brake: a spring provides the braking force, and when power is applied, the magnet attracts the armature, releasing the brake.

Because the spring applies braking force the instant power is lost, this brake acts as a safety brake that stops the load safely on power loss or fuse cutting. It is suitable for vertical-load systems such as hoists.

### Key Functions

- Fail-safe operation: automatic braking by spring force on power loss
- When energized, the magnet attracts the armature, releasing the brake and allowing shaft rotation
- Safely stops the load during power failure or fuse cutting

### Operating Principle

**RELEASING OF THE BRAKE**
When the magnet(1) is energized, the moving armature(2) is attracted to the magnet(1), and the spring(3) is compressed. As a result, the pressure on the facing (lining)(4) is diminished, the brake is released, the shaft begins to revolve, and the hoisting or lowering operation starts.

**BRAKING**
When the current is turned off, the attractive force of the magnet(1) is lost. At the same time, the spring pushes the armature toward the facing (lining), returning it to its former position. As a result, braking force is applied to the facing, the shaft stops revolving, and the hoisting/lowering operation stops.
