## Troubleshooting

Search keywords: SLIP, GAP, lining contamination, insufficient braking torque, spring

### Symptom: SLIP (delayed/incomplete braking) occurs during use

**Possible Cause**
- The GAP between MAGNET(1) and ARMATURE(2) has widened beyond the 0.5mm spec (e.g. due to lining wear)

**Inspection**
- [ ] Measure the GAP around the full 360° circumference and check whether it exceeds 0.5mm

**Corrective Action**
1. Loosen the four GAP FIXING BOLT(5) locations and re-adjust the GAP to 0.5mm using the GAP REGULATION SCREW(6)
2. Re-tighten the GAP FIXING BOLT(5) locations evenly

**Final Check**
Confirm the shaft rotates without resistance in a no-load trial run, then confirm SLIP does not recur in a loaded trial run.

---

### Symptom: Reduced braking force due to lining contamination

**Possible Cause**
- Oil or moisture has penetrated the LINING(4) surface

**Inspection**
- [ ] Visually inspect the lining surface for contamination, discoloration, or moisture

**Corrective Action**
1. First eliminate the contamination source (e.g. an oil leak point)
2. Clean the lining surface (if contamination is severe or the lining itself is damaged, follow the relevant brake manufacturer's replacement procedure — a lining replacement procedure is not included in this material)

### Symptom: Insufficient braking torque (brakes, but weakly)

**Possible Cause**
- TORQUE ADJUSTABLE BOLT(7) under-adjusted

**Inspection**
- [ ] Check current braking condition and the tightness of TORQUE ADJUSTABLE BOLT(7)

**Corrective Action**
1. Turn the TORQUE ADJUSTABLE BOLT(7) clockwise (CW) little by little to increase braking force
2. Re-check braking condition with a trial run after each adjustment

> ℹ A specific tightening amount (torque value) is not given in the provided material — adjust in small increments on-site while checking braking condition. Over-tightening can cause a sudden braking shock, so proceed with caution.

---

### Symptom: Brake stays released even when de-energized (power OFF) — no braking

**Possible Cause**
- SPRING(3) is defective or broken
- Poor adjustment of GAP FIXING BOLT(5)/GAP REGULATION SCREW(6), preventing the ARMATURE from returning to its original position

**Inspection**
- [ ] Visually confirm the ARMATURE fully returns toward the LINING when power is OFF
- [ ] Check the SPRING(3) for visible damage

**Corrective Action**
1. First re-perform the GAP adjustment procedure (Setting tab) to rule out mechanical binding
2. If spring damage is suspected, stop operation immediately and evaluate part replacement (a spring replacement procedure is not included in this material — check with the manufacturer)

> ⚠ Failure to brake in the de-energized state is a critical fault that can directly lead to a load-drop accident. If the cause cannot be confirmed, stop operation on that shaft immediately and implement separate fall-prevention measures.
